# Aimi Privacy Policy

Oct 28, 2026 · @Ray

**Effective date:** 11/29/2026

Aimi is a personal assistant app for your goals, fitness, meals, bills and news. It is developed and operated by Randy Imbia. This policy explains what information Aimi collects, how it is used and shared, and the choices you have.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not show ads.

## Information we collect

We collect only what you enter or what the app needs to work.

| Type | What it includes |
| --- | --- |
| Account | Name, email address, password (stored only in scrambled, hashed form), time zone, currency and units. If you sign in with Google or Apple, the account ID and email address they share with us. |
| Your tracking data | Business and fitness goals, workouts, fitness plans, body details you choose to enter (height, weight, target weight), meals and nutrition estimates, bills, payment dates, payday and income if you enter them, music albums (song titles and links), and notes. |
| Assistant data | Your messages to the AI assistant, its replies, and the facts it remembers about you (for example "prefers morning workouts"). |
| Preferences | News interests, notification settings, reminder times and the city you enter for weather. |
| Device data | A push notification token so we can send notifications to your phone. |
| Activity history | A log of changes in the app (for example "Bill paid"), shown to you in Settings. |

Some of this is health information (body details, workouts, meals) or financial information (bills, income). We treat it as sensitive and use it only to provide the app's features to you.

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, photos or browsing history.

## How we use your information

We use your information to run Aimi for you:

- Show, store and sync your goals, workouts, meals, bills and news.
- Let the AI assistant answer you and make the changes you ask for.
- Create suggestions, plans, summaries and charts, such as meal ideas, workout plans, bill tips, the daily brief and the weekly review.
- Send the reminders and notifications you turn on.
- Keep your account secure and fix problems.

### The AI assistant

When you chat with Aimi or use an AI feature, the relevant parts of your data are sent to our AI provider (OpenAI) to generate a response. This can include your message, recent messages in the conversation, facts Aimi remembers about you, and a summary of your goals, workouts, meals and bills.

Aimi remembers useful facts from your conversations to make future suggestions more personal. You can view, edit and delete these memories in Settings under "What the AI knows".

AI suggestions about health, fitness, food and money are general information, not professional medical, nutritional or financial advice. Nutrition values are estimates.

## Services that process data for us

We share data only with the service providers that run parts of Aimi, and only what each one needs.

| Provider | What it does | Data it handles |
| --- | --- | --- |
| MongoDB Atlas | Database hosting | All account and app data |
| Render | Runs Aimi's server | All data passing through the app |
| OpenAI | Powers the AI assistant and AI features | The data described under "The AI assistant" |
| Google (Firebase Cloud Messaging) | Delivers push notifications on Android | Device token and notification text |
| Apple (Push Notification service) | Delivers push notifications on iPhone | Device token and notification text |
| Google and Apple sign-in | Let you sign in with those accounts | Account ID and email address |
| Weather service | Weather for workout suggestions | The city you enter, never your name |
| Food databases (USDA FoodData Central, Open Food Facts) | Nutrition values and barcode lookups | Food names and barcodes only |
| Apple and Google app stores | Payments for subscriptions, if offered | Purchase details; we never see your card number |

OpenAI's handling of data sent through its API is described in [OpenAI's privacy and data policies](https://openai.com/policies). We may also disclose information if required by law.

## What we never do

- Sell or rent your personal information.
- Use your data for advertising or share it with advertisers.
- Use your health or financial information for anything other than providing Aimi to you.

## How long we keep your data

We keep your data while your account is active. When you delete your account, we delete your account and all its data from our database. Some data may remain for a short time in our providers' systems (for example, logs) before they delete it under their own policies. News articles that are shared by all users are not personal data and are removed after about 90 days unless saved.

## Your choices and rights

- **See and correct:** view and edit your data in the app at any time.
- **Export:** download a copy of your data from Settings, or ask us by email.
- **Delete:** delete individual items, or your whole account in Settings > Delete account. You can also ask us by email.
- **AI memories:** view, edit or delete what Aimi remembers about you in Settings.
- **Notifications:** turn each type on or off in Settings or in your phone's settings.

Depending on where you live, you may have further rights under local law, such as the right to object to or limit certain processing. Contact us to use them.

## Security

We protect your data with encrypted connections (HTTPS), hashed passwords, short-lived sign-in tokens, strict separation so each account can only access its own data, and limits on repeated requests. No system is completely secure, but we work to protect your information and will notify you as required by law if a breach affects you.

## Children

Aimi is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

## Where your data is processed

Our providers may store and process data in the United States and other countries. By using Aimi, you understand that your information may be processed outside your country.

## Changes to this policy

If we make important changes, we will update the effective date above and let you know in the app.
